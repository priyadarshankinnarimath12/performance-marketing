## Mygate

### Objective
Drive qualified acquisition across **Mygate Homes, CEP campaigns, and Middle East markets**.

### Channels
**Meta Ads · Google Ads · LinkedIn Ads**

### Execution
- Managed **₹15L+** in paid media across Meta, Google & LinkedIn
- Analysed creative and campaign signals to identify winning patterns
- Used performance signals to launch and iterate new campaigns across channels
- Built acquisition campaigns for **property listings, B2B leads, and Middle East demand generation**
- Optimised campaigns based on **lead quality, conversion performance, and funnel behaviour**

### Results
- **200+ property listings** generated for Mygate Homes
- **350+ B2B leads** generated for CEP campaigns
- **250+ MQLs** generated across Middle East markets
- **50+ demos** generated from Middle East MQLs
- **₹15L+ budget** managed across CEP, Mygate Homes, and Property Management Software - Middle East
